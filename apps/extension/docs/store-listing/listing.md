## Name

Solid

## Short description

Sign in to your Solid accounts, choose which sites can use each account, and manage access from one browser panel.

## Detailed description

Keep your Solid accounts together in your browser and choose what you share with each site.
Solid lets you keep your account and data with providers you choose. This extension connects
supported websites to those accounts after you approve access.

- Add up to eight accounts, give them useful names, and choose a default account.
- Choose which account a site can use. Your work and personal sites can use different accounts
  at the same time.
- Review a site's request before sharing. The approval window shows the account, the data
  requested, the actions allowed, and when access ends.
- Open the side panel to see sites with access, review their permissions, shorten access,
  change the connected account, or revoke access. Changing an account requires fresh approval
  before the site can read that account's data.
- Fill contact and address forms from your profile. Click the small button on a form field,
  or right-click it, review the exact values in an extension window, and choose what to fill.
  Nothing is filled or submitted without your choice, and password and payment fields are
  never touched.
- Use the toolbar menu for quick account controls, or keep the side panel open while browsing.
- Remove an account from the extension when you no longer want to use it here.

Signing in does not automatically share your account with every website. A site receives
your account address and approved data only after you give it access. Sign-in tokens and
private security keys stay inside the extension's background storage.

You need an existing Solid account and a supported website. This extension does not create
an account or host your data. Removing an account from the extension leaves the account and
its files with your provider.

There are no analytics, advertisements, or sales of your data.
